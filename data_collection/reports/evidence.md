# Published corridor and policy evidence catalog (2026-09-26)

`data_collection/evidence/collect.py` creates the tiny independent shard `data_collection/work/evidence.sqlite` (24,576 bytes). Its three official sources, eight claims and exact source locators are in `evidence_sources` and `evidence_claims`. Downloaded HTML and XLSX files with checksum manifests total about 452 KB under ignored `data_collection/work/evidence_cache/`. Run `backend/.venv/bin/python data_collection/evidence/collect.py` and `backend/.venv/bin/python data_collection/evidence/verify.py`.

Four claims summarize published UNODC World Drug Report 2026 sections: [Cocaine trafficking](https://data.unodc.org/wdr2026?page=51) and [Methamphetamine trafficking flows](https://data.unodc.org/wdr2026?page=17). The cocaine section describes reported Colombia/Ecuador departures serving North America and Europe and Brazil-linked departures toward Europe, Africa and Asia over **2020–2024**. The methamphetamine section ranks Afghanistan as a reported departure country in Asia and Mexico in the Americas over **2021–2024**. These are source statements about reported departure patterns and multiyear regional groupings. They are **not** bilateral country-pair measurements, annual flow volumes, route-specific health exposures, or evidence that a departure country is a cultivation origin. `evidence_claims` records source excerpt, section/bullet locator, publication year, observation window, normalized claim, geography and this caveat for each.

Four policy milestones come from [UNODC WDR 2026 annex table 11.1](https://www.unodc.org/documents/data-and-analysis/WDR_2026/Annex/11.1_cannabis_regulation_in_different_countries_and_jurisdictions.xlsx), with original worksheet cell locator and text: Canada federal implementation on **17 October 2018**, Malta implementation on **18 December 2021**, Luxembourg's limited reform on **21 July 2023**, and Germany's Cannabis Act in **April 2024** (grower clubs in July). The annex records different scopes of legal access; none should be converted to a generic unrestricted retail legalization flag. The record is a published UNODC regulatory compilation reviewed in 2024–2025 and released in the 2026 annex, not an independent legal audit. Any causal policy analysis should verify the jurisdiction-specific operative date and relevant outcome definition. Uruguay was left out because the annex's “Date passed” cell appears inconsistent with the named law; the catalog does not silently resolve that conflict.

`verify.py` checks SQLite integrity, foreign keys, source file SHA-256 hashes, route excerpts against downloaded official pages, exact claim counts, window years and policy-date presence. The catalog does not provide public route ground truth. The public IDS fields in this project identify seizure location but not observed origin/transit/destination for each case; the WDR statements can annotate candidate corridors and explain scenarios, but cannot validate a year-by-year route forecast or create synthetic bilateral edges. No enforcement vulnerability information is collected.
