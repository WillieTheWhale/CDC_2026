Fixtures `route_evidence.json`, `route_evidence_item.json`. | No (additive) |
| 2026-09-27 | Evidence drilldown: `GET /api/evidence/values`, `/api/evidence/value/{value_id}` (research or market value → formula → inputs → original cells and source), `/api/evidence/health/{iso3}`, `/api/evidence/overdose` and `/api/evidence/research-model`. Fixtures `evidence_values.json`, `evidence_value.json`, `evidence_value_market.json`, `evidence_health.json`, `evidence_overdose.json`, `evidence_research_model.json`. | No (additive) |

New fixtures `evidence_market_observations.json`, `evidence_market_observation_countries.json`; `evidence_values.json` and `evidence_health.json` regenerated. OpenAPI fragment pending merge. | No (additive) |
