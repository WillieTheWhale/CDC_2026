## Source, method, and reuse

- [DOJ News API](https://www.justice.gov/developer/api-documentation/api_v1): `GET /api/v1/press_releases.json` with `parameters[title]=drug trafficking`, 50 results per page, date descending. DOJ documents `uuid`, title filtering, pagination and a warning that more than four requests per second may degrade service or be blocked. The importer spaces requests by at least 350 ms, retries 429/5xx, checkpoints each page/attachment outside the repo, and deduplicates by release UUID and exact candidate span. It caps documents, pages and scanned PDF attachments. HTML image tags, including embedded base64 images, are removed from the temporary checkpoint without affecting text extraction.
- [DOJ legal policies](https://www.justice.gov/legalpolicies) say DOJ website information is generally public domain unless otherwise indicated, and request source citation. They specifically caution that third-party photos, graphics and other materials may have separate copyright. No images are imported; file-level rights and identity would need review before any future portrait use.
- Reproduce from the repository root with `node frontend/scripts/import-doj-people-review.mjs --output frontend/data/people/candidates/doj-drug-trafficking-corpus-2026-09-26.json --state /tmp/trace-doj-full-drug-trafficking-2026-09-26.json --terms 'drug trafficking' --max-documents 9000 --max-pages-per-term 180 --max-attachments 30`. Add `--resume` with the same checkpoint to replay or continue. `pdftotext` is required for eligible PDF rosters. Keep the checkpoint, which contains full fetched DOJ bodies, outside the repo. These are discovery matches only. This purposive sample is not a statistical estimate of precision or recall, and it does not establish that legal statuses remain current.

The title filter misses releases under other wording; the conservative sentence matcher misses many paragraph forms; older releases can be superseded by later outcomes; repeated names are not identity-resolved; and PDF column extraction can fuse names. Before publishing a person, a human must verify the complete identity, exact individual allegation or adjudicated outcome, event date, current status and as-of date, explicit network role/edge, historical country-level operation, and any image's original rights. **Automatically publishable profiles: zero.**
