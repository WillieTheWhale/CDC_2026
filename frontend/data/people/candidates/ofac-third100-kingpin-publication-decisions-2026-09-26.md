The linked Treasury release names the individual. Exact/alias/token-order deduplication against the published People manifest found no accepted matches. Dates of birth were used only for identity verification and are not copied into published profiles.

This review does not claim uninterrupted listing, prove that no intervening update occurred, or infer criminal guilt, organization membership, a particular drug, or an operating country from program, address, nationality, or co-listing.
