# DOJ expanded document review queue — 2026-09-26

This is a **discovery queue, not published People data**. `doj-expanded-pilot-2026-09-26.json` stays under `candidates/` and is not imported into the People manifest. A name in a release, charge table, or co-defendant roster establishes neither current legal status nor organization membership, an individual role, a person-to-person edge, an operating country, or an event date. Charges are allegations. No photos, addresses, birth dates, coordinates, or article bodies are stored in this output.

## Source, reuse, and reproducibility

- [DOJ News API](https://www.justice.gov/developer/api-documentation/api_v1): `/api/v1/press_releases.json`, `parameters[title]`, up to 50 results/page. The API documentation warns that more than four requests per second may degrade service; the importer waits at least 350 ms between requests and retries 429/5xx responses. It is bounded by document, page-per-term, and PDF attachment caps, checkpoints each page and attachment, deduplicates article UUIDs and exact syndicated candidate rows, and resumes with `--resume`.
- [DOJ legal policies](https://www.justice.gov/legalpolicies): site information is generally public domain unless marked otherwise. The output retains brief source spans for review. Photographs and attached documents may have distinct rights; this pilot imports no images. The one processed attachment is the [official Weymouth defendant/charge PDF](https://www.justice.gov/media/1416691/dl), cited by its [DOJ release](https://www.justice.gov/usao-edpa/pr/33-alleged-members-violent-kensington-drug-trafficking-organization-charged-41-count). Its extracted names require visual PDF-layout verification.
- From the repository root, a fresh bounded run is `node frontend/scripts/import-doj-people-review.mjs --output frontend/data/people/candidates/doj-expanded-pilot-2026-09-26.json --state /tmp/trace-doj-crawl-2026-09-26-v2.json`. Pass `--resume` to regenerate from an existing checkpoint. The checkpoint contains full fetched DOJ bodies and belongs outside the repository. `pdftotext` is required to review PDF rosters. A later fresh crawl may differ; the committed JSON fixes this pilot's observations.
