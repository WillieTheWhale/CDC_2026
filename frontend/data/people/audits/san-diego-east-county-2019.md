## Interpretation limits

The eight organizations are **case-based alleged networks**, one per indictment. DOJ describes them as linked to the Sinaloa Cartel, but does not identify each named defendant as a direct cartel member. Co-defendant listing does not create a person-to-person graph edge, so this fixture has zero pairwise connections.

Every profile's cited individual count alleges conduct “within the Southern District of California and elsewhere,” or a dated offense there. Its single USA country marker means a **2019 alleged U.S. case activity association** only. It does not represent residence, physical presence at a specific site, a current position, or guilt. The release's “hometown” column and general allegations about remittances to Mexico do not justify an individual Mexico operating marker.

The published charge status is explicitly **as of September 19, 2019**, when DOJ announced the indictments were unsealed. Searches of official DOJ material using the eight leaders’ names and the case numbers did not establish later individual dispositions as of this review. This is a limitation of the review, not evidence that cases remain open or that these people are currently wanted. No portraits were added without separate file-level reuse verification.
