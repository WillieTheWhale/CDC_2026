## Exclusions and appellate limits

- Trần Văn Hưng is held: the government article reports an earlier life drug sentence and a later combined life term, but does not allocate the 2015 case’s 27-year component by offense or give the earlier judgment date.
- The Hòa Bình government article reports five bribery-mediation convictions and one failure-to-report conviction. Dương Ngô Sơn, Nguyễn Quang Nho, Nguyễn Quang Viên and Nguyễn Văn Tới are excluded as bribery-only. Nguyễn Thị Thủy in that case is excluded as failure-to-report-only; it is not conflated with the separate same-name defendant in the 2014 Quảng Ninh life-sentence roster. Dương Thị Khang is included because the article expressly gives her a separate 20-year drug conviction.
- Quảng Ninh: the government first-instance article names all 30 death and 13 life defendants. The government appeal article says 29 death sentences were affirmed and one was reduced; [Tuổi Trẻ names Nông Văn Len as the sole reduction](https://tuoitre.vn/vu-mua-ban-4400-banh-heroin-con-29-an-tu-hinh-613674.htm). The other 29 appeal outcomes are explicit cross-source inference from that exhaustive list, stated as such. The 13 life defendants’ individual appeal outcomes are unallocated and retained as first-instance snapshots.
- Hòa Bình: [Báo Công lý’s 2016 appellate report](https://congly.vn/vu-an-trum-ma-tuy-vu-ngoc-son-y-an-tu-hinh-8-doi-tuong-mua-ban-541-banh-heroin-14383.html) identifies seven upheld death sentences, Nguyễn Thị Thịnh’s withdrawn appeal, Trần Thị Mai’s upheld life sentence, and Triệu Văn Đời’s withdrawn appeal. It also identifies drug convictions for Đào Trọng Kiên and Lương Văn Lằng. Other life defendants’ appeal outcomes remain unverified.
- Nguyễn Thị Bích Ngọc is the full-name form of Nguyễn Bích Ngọc in the same 2014 government article; Vũ Đình Sơn is expressly given as Vũ Ngọc Sơn’s alternate name. Hoàng Tuyết Lan is a later court-newspaper spelling variant of Hoàng Thị Tuyết Lan. No other short-name matches are assumed.
- Only Vũ Ngọc Sơn and Nguyễn Thị Bích Ngọc have personal historical VNM conduct evidence. Heroin is person-assigned to those two and Nguyễn Xuân Hòa; all other drug arrays are empty despite case-wide heroin and synthetic-drug figures. No photographs copied.
