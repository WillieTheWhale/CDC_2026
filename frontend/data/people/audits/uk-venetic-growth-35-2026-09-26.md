- Daryll Hall was sentenced in his absence on 7 January 2025; the NCA release does not establish later capture or present custody.

The March 2025 Byrne release gives only January 2024 for Chris O’Shea’s sentence, and the November 2025 McKenzie release gives only February 2024 for Wayne McKenzie’s sentence. Their source-date development events explicitly avoid fabricating an exact sentencing day.
- The CPS Donovan release identifies four people convicted of money laundering only: Christopher Roper, James Vaughan, Kenneth Kean and Paul Duncan. Kean and Duncan received suspended sentences. These profiles do not claim drug-trafficking convictions or assign a drug type based solely on group activity.
- Peter Robinson is documented as hiding cash for Bennett’s drug enterprise and pleading guilty; the NCA release does not state his precise count. His profile describes a cash-handling role without claiming a named drug conviction. Richard Small, Gerald Tohill, Lucien Robinson and Wayne McKenzie likewise have no specific drug label where the source describes their roles without identifying their individual drug types.
- Exact dated arrest events are included only when the release gives the day. Where it supplies only a month or the publication date of a later account, the profile does not invent a day. Legal status is historical as of the source date and says nothing about later appeals or current custody.
