## Reproduction notes

The reviewed cohort is 100 accepted records across action years 2004 (5), 2008 (8), 2012 (10), 2013 (16), 2014 (17), 2015 (11), 2016 (12), 2017 (12), and 2018 (9). Nine accepted 2018 rows were held in the earlier bounded review only because they were outside its publication tranche. The three hold rows were deliberately inspected but excluded. Names were deduplicated against the published People manifest by OFAC UID and normalized primary/alias tokens. The manifest and region audit are regenerated separately.

The original dated action is the citation for the sanction event, and the current CSV is the citation for present list status. The annual archive corroborates the historical addition; it is not used to establish present status. The XML `Created` event is an original listing date, not a criminal-case date.
