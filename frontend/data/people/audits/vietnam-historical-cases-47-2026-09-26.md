# Vietnam historical drug case source and claim audit

Reviewed 2026-09-26. **47 individually named profiles** from six 2017–2020 court-case cohorts; 47 historic Vietnam country associations; one directly stated spousal relation; zero portraits. All source material is linked per row. A conviction here means the cited sentencing stage only, not final appellate result, present custody, or that a death sentence was carried out. Cohort organizations identify court proceedings and are not independent cartel designations. No Laos, China, or Cambodia person-country marker was inferred from a reported transnational case.

The 2017 Hà Nội case excludes Vi Văn Tuấn: a [2018 VnExpress account](https://vnexpress.net/nguoi-dan-ong-trong-duong-day-buon-nghin-banh-heroin-bi-tuyen-an-tu-3828347.html) documents a later retrial, so the original life-sentence report would be stale without further resolution. The Quảng Ninh report names Đỗ Quang Vinh and Nguyễn Thị Hương as receiving 20-year sentences, but its paragraph does not clearly attribute their individual offense among a multi-offense case; both are held. The Hà Nam report names only four of 22 defendants with individual outcomes; unnamed people are not added.

## Reconciliation notes

- The Cao Bằng prosecutorial report dates its first-instance judgment **9 March 2020** and a prosecutorial appeal **6 April 2020** for Phan Văn Hiển, Triệu Văn Tình, and Nguyễn Văn Quyến. Their profile status is time-bound to 8 April 2020, when that appeal was reported. The appeal result is unverified. The report states Quyến received 20 years for the drug-trading count; local reporting gives 27 years across multiple offenses. The profile uses the narrower prosecutor claim.
- The 2017 Sài Gòn Giải Phóng article spells Lục Văn Thượng; another local report spells the given-name ending differently. This batch retains the spelling used by its directly linked source and does not assign an unsupported alias.
- The 2018 Hà Nội and Hà Nam articles do not state a calendar day of judgment independently of publication. Their event rows are labeled court sentence **reported**, with the publication date, rather than a guessed verdict date.
- The 2018 Quảng Ninh government article explicitly identifies Hoàng Văn Tiến and Trần Thu Hằng as husband and wife and co-leaders of one branch. The sole direct edge is limited to that documented relationship. Other co-defendants receive no inferred person-to-person edge.
- Deduplication checked exact accent-folded names and aliases against the 964-profile manifest before this batch; no match. This is not proof that every common Vietnamese name uniquely identifies one real-world person.
