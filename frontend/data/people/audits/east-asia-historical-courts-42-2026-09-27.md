## Holds and limitations

- Hong Kong government police releases found during review often suppressed defendant names; unnamed adults and minors were not included.
- South Korean and Japanese drug reports located in this pass generally anonymized defendant identities or named only performers in use/possession cases; these were not treated as network people.
- A 2017 CNA report of five Taiwanese nationals convicted in the Philippines was held from the Taiwan cohort: citizenship alone does not establish Taiwan drug-case conduct.
- The Thai PBS case names นิมิตร เป็งศิริ, but states that the trial court acquitted him. He was excluded. The article also narrows the quantity attributable to พรพรรณ รัตนเมธานนท์ at final review; her profile reports that narrowed result.
- The 2019 Shaoyang source does not allocate exact individual sentences among the last five named defendants. Their profiles state only the reported ten-to-fifteen-year range, not an invented individual term. Appeals were not verified.
- Four 2010 Supreme People’s Court death sentences were reported as executed before the 2010-12-13 publication, without exact execution dates. No execution event date was invented.
- No portrait was added because no individual image license or public-domain basis was established.
- These court cohorts are source-defined case groupings, not independently designated cartels. Unspecified co-defendant relationships were not represented as person edges.
