## Held or excluded

- **M. Rizki** appears only as an initial plus common given name in the [Cirebon report](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/606174/@AntaraKalbar); identity is too ambiguous for a public profile. Hendri Unan and Gunawan Aminah received money-laundering sentences in the same case and are excluded from drug-conviction claims; the report does not state Fajar Priyo Susilo’s final sentence.
- **Kim Que Yu** was explicitly acquitted by the [2002 Philippine Supreme Court decision](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/51039). **Li Wien Shien** was acquitted in the [Lagman–Zeng case](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/48177); no guilt is inferred from presence at the laboratory. **Menda Aneslag** and **Mae Elarmo** were acquitted in the [Aneslag–Concepcion case](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/55327).
- In [Robert Uy v. People](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/68872), the Supreme Court reversed the appealed conviction. Robert Uy and co-defendants named in the lower-court judgment were excluded. In [People v. Wilson D. Lim](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/51355), four trial defendants were expressly acquitted on appeal; all are excluded.
- The [Alberto–Vargas opinion](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/67217) says the claimed West African Drug Syndicate membership of Vargas was hearsay. The two named people are included only for their affirmed heroin offense, with no syndicate membership label or edge.
- Bali Nine status is not modeled as current custody after the 2024 transfer; the Australian Government source confirms return and rehabilitation opportunity but does not settle later supervision terms. The Indonesian and Philippine sources provide no rights clearance for portraits, so none was copied.
- The present batch does not add Thai or Malaysian records: the reviewed source hits did not produce a safely matched historical person-specific drug outcome and country claim.

## Deduplication and date checks

Exact accent-folded names and documented aliases were compared with the then-current published manifest; none matched. Each Philippine Supreme Court outcome date comes from the judgment header and dispositive paragraph. The 2017 Cirebon report explicitly dates the verdict 11 January 2017. The Palembang source gives a report date of 8 February 2019, while describing the verdict as Thursday; its timeline item is intentionally a **reporting** event dated 8 February, not a guessed judgment day. ANTARA’s 17 February 2006 Bali Nine article reports that the trial sentences occurred during the preceding week, so those events are also dated as reports.
